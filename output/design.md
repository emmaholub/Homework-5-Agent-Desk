# Campus Customs Operations Dashboard

- **Agent desk layout:** Three columns separate the queue, active ticket, and
  human controls so an operator can scan the whole workflow quickly.
- **Yale blue with coral and mint accents:** The palette feels connected to the
  Campus Customs brand while using coral for attention and mint for completed or
  approved states.
- **Live agent rail:** Each worker has a visible status dot and short statement,
  making the handoff sequence understandable without exposing internal secrets.
- **Human control card:** The payment button is visually distinct and explicitly
  says that agents cannot move money. The balance is always visible and refreshes
  after approval.
- **Ticket cards and responsive layout:** Ticket selection is clear on desktop
  and collapses into a readable single-column view on smaller screens.
- **Recent activity feed:** Operators can see which agent spoke and which MCP
  tools were attributed to the step, supporting quick operational review.
