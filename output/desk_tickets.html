Reflection

The corrected run was safe and selective. The Boss routed 101 to Customer Service and Inventory, 102 to Facilities and Accounting, and 103 to Customer Service, Inventory, and Accounting.

1. How did the agents do?

Ticket 101 found size S at 0 and a human approved the linked $840 invoice. Ticket 102 verified the lease and prepared the $2,400 rent payment for human approval. Ticket 103 found 8 size-M hoodies, $58 list price, and $22 cost without inventing a discount. The starting $3,400 minus $840 and $2,400 equals the database ending balance of $160.

2. Actual vs. Expected

Actual now matches the selective plans on the ticket tabs. The Boss no longer calls every specialist in a fixed order, Facilities uses the leases table, and only human approval moves money.

3. What would have been simpler as one agent?

A single operations agent with the three MCP tools could handle these tickets with fewer handoffs. The human payment button should remain separate because it is a permission and safety boundary.

4. Three new problems it could solve

  • Explain overdue invoice 501 and prepare its payment.
  • Check whether the navy hoodie comes in L and report its stock and price.
  • Compare cost versus list price for a human-selected discount.

5. Three new problems it could not solve

  • Order more stock because MCP is read-only.
  • Message customers because there is no email tool or contact table.
  • Forecast cash because there is no sales data.
